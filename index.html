                  Backup and PIN

                  Your backup file contains everything: ledger, debts, savings, accounts, wedding fund and tax tracker.

                  Choose a backup file, then press OK to restore it.

                  Downloads work when the app is opened from your own website, not inside a preview.

                  Install on your phone

                  Android (Chrome): open the menu (⋮), then Install app.

                  iPhone (Safari): tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

                  Save a backup first, because the installed app can keep its own separate data. Then use Restore backup inside the installed app.

                  Your data saves automatically on this device and in this browser. Clearing browser data or switching devices may remove or hide it, so keep a backup for extra safety.
